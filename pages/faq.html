Got Questions?

We've Got Answers 🎁

Browse the most common questions below.

What makes IGötNag different from other gift sites? +

Simple — we don't sell greeting cards! IGötNag is all about real, tangible gifts that people will actually keep, use, and love. Every product on our site can be personalised with a name, photo, message or custom design. We're the anti-card gift shop. 🎁

How does personalisation work? +

Simply select the product you'd like, click "Customise Now", and follow the steps to add your name, photo, message or choose a design. You'll see a preview before you place your order. Our team handles the rest!

How fast is delivery? +

Standard UK delivery is 3–5 working days. Express delivery (1–2 working days) is available at checkout. Orders over £30 qualify for free standard delivery. International shipping is also available — check checkout for rates.

What is your returns policy? +

We offer a 30-day return policy on non-personalised items. Because personalised items are made especially for you, we cannot accept returns unless the product arrives damaged or with an error on our part. If anything arrives wrong, contact us immediately — we'll sort it! 😊

How do I track my order? +

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive a confirmation email with your tracking number. You can also log into your account and view your order status under "My Orders".

Can I add gift wrapping to my order? +

Yes! We offer beautiful custom gift wrapping on all orders — free of charge. Simply select the gift wrapping option at checkout and add any personal message you'd like included.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. All payments are SSL encrypted and secure.

My product arrived damaged – what do I do? +

We're so sorry to hear that! Please contact us at info@igotnag.com with a photo of the damage within 48 hours of delivery. We'll send a replacement or offer a full refund — no questions asked.

Do you offer Print-on-Demand products? +

Yes! We're integrating with Printful and Printify, allowing you to upload a photo or design and get it printed on mugs, cushions, t-shirts, phone covers, and more. This feature will be fully live soon — watch this space! ✨

I sold, do you sell greeting cards? +

Absolutely NOT. 🚫🎴 That's literally our whole thing. We are Not Another Greeting-card — N.A.G. We only sell real, tangible gifts that people love. No cards. Ever. You're welcome. 😎
